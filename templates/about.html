The Story Behind NeuroTraff

With increasing congestion in Thane district, daily commuters face unpredictable delays, stress, and lost productivity. NeuroTraff was born from a simple yet powerful vision: What if we could predict traffic before it happens?

This project leverages machine learning and open data to predict real-time traffic levels, empowering commuters to make smarter travel decisions. Whether you're planning your morning commute or scheduling a business meeting, NeuroTraff helps you arrive on time, every time.
